# What viral posts actually SAY and SHOW — copyable formats for Floored

Second research pass: verbatim hooks, post structures and accounts worth copying, with the 34 hook lines adapted for Floored.

Research pass, 2026-09-10. Scope: verbatim hooks, beat-by-beat structures, sounds, comment-bait, accounts. Mechanics (slide counts, safe zones, fonts) deliberately not repeated.

**Honesty up front — where evidence is thin:**
- TikTok's own pages (`/discover/...`, `/@user/video/...`) do not render for a fetch, so every TikTok caption quoted below comes from search-result snippets or third-party write-ups. View counts are as reported by those sources, not verified in-app.
- No reliable example found of a viral **"my AI did X" / AI-narrator** post for a *game*. The closest real things are (a) Matt Shumer's and Pieter Levels' X posts (human voice, AI credited), and (b) the AI-history "POV: You wake up in…" format (AI-made visuals, not an AI persona). Treat "AI-perspective" as untested.
- No reliable example of an indie-game **"guess the number / guess how long this took"** post with numbers. The format is documented generically (Blitzcut's "How much do you think this cost?", OpenClip's "comment your number"), not as a proven game-dev hit.
- No reliable, cited **"fixed it / before-after" game-dev** post with numbers. Every marketing guide names the format as top-performing; none could point to a specific post.
- JDM/night-drive TikTok *account* follower counts are mostly unverified; the accounts are real, the size claims are not confirmed.

### 1d. "Browser game / no download" flex

- fly.pieter.com: "no registration or downloads" was in every write-up; the *flex* was "made in 3 hours, already 26K online" ([levels.io](https://levels.io/fly-pieter-com-vibecoded-flight-simulator)).

## 3. What keeps people swiping to the end

Sourced patterns (OpenClip, SlideStorm, Creou, Grow Creator, Neal Schaffer):

- **Write the last slide first.** "One emotional beat per slide, twist or payoff on the final slide" ([OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/tiktok-slideshow-ideas)).
- **Slide 2 must be the strongest.** "Make slide two the strongest tip, not slide one… slide two proves the list is worth finishing" (same). For Floored: slide 1 hook text over a quiet frame, slide 2 the single best-looking frame you have.
- **Swipe-back loop.** Pose a question on slide 1, answer it on the last slide in a way that makes people swipe back to slide 1 ([SlideStorm](https://www.slidestorm.ai/articles/tiktok-slideshow-hooks)). Example: slide 1 "one of these frames is a real dashcam" → last slide "it was slide 1."
- **Visual loop.** Last slide near-identical to slide 1 so the auto-play wraps seamlessly (Creou; same trick as Shorts "last 2 seconds flow into first 2" — [Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/gaming-shorts-retention-tips)).
- **Countdown / escalation.** "Escalating from mild to unhinged" ordering; "5 things… one lesson per slide" with numbers counting down ([OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/tiktok-slideshow-ideas)).
- **Open loop → part two.** "Open-ended cliffhangers forcing part two" — but only when part one delivers standalone value; "Part 1 of ∞" without payoff is flagged dead ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-tiktok-hooks-2026)).
- **Hidden-object.** "Find the hidden [thing]" / Megabonk's count-the-bonks: forces a full pass through every slide.
- **Leave a question unanswered on purpose** so the comments finish it; then post the answer within 24h (Schrödinger's Cat Burglar, [Acorn](https://acorngames.gg/blog/2025/8/10/the-indie-devs-guide-to-mastering-tiktok-in-2025)).
- **End funny sets on the joke, not a CTA** ([OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/tiktok-slideshow-ideas)); put key art / game name in the final frame so people can find it ([Acorn](https://acorngames.gg/blog/2025/8/10/the-indie-devs-guide-to-mastering-tiktok-in-2025)).

Concrete Floored 7-slide skeleton that uses all of it:
1. Quiet cockpit frame + "one of these is real dashcam footage"
2. Best frame (rain, tail lights) — no text
3. Tunnel — "3"
4. Neon overpass — "2"
5. Deliberate flaw frame — "1 (find the bug)"
6. "none of them. AI wrote the code. no download." + URL
7. Same frame as slide 1, text: "swipe back."

## 5. Video specifics (7–15s)

- **7–15s is the target for gameplay highlights** ([insights.gg](https://insights.gg/blog/how-to-post-gaming-clips-to-tiktok)); under 20s gets 42% completion on Shorts ([Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/youtube-shorts-looping/)).
- **First frame:** open mid-action, never on a menu, logo, black frame or the *climax* ("start on the parry windup, not the riposte"). "Lives or dies on the first half-second of motion." ([Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/gaming-shorts-retention-tips)). If the moment needs build-up, put the payoff in the first second, then show it in full ([insights.gg](https://insights.gg/blog/how-to-post-gaming-clips-to-tiktok)).
- **Text on first frame, because feeds start muted.** ≤5 words, on screen by ~0.5s, resolved by 2s. Examples used: "1v4 with 8 HP", "this shouldn't have worked", "Have you found this?" ([insights.gg](https://insights.gg/blog/how-to-post-gaming-clips-to-tiktok), [Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/gaming-shorts-retention-tips)).
- **Should text change mid-clip?** Yes, but one idea at a time, staggered, never overlapping; 5–10 words/second max ([Riverside](https://riverside.com/blog/how-to-add-text-to-tiktok-videos), [Coinis](https://coinis.com/how-to/best-way-to-text-overlay-tiktok-ad)). For a 10s Floored clip: line 1 at 0s (hook), line 2 at ~4s (the twist: "no download" / "AI wrote it"), nothing in the last 2s so the loop is clean.
- **Speed ramps:** ramp on the loudest beat; CapCut "Smooth" curve; export 1080p60 ([Alight Motions guide](https://thealightmotions.com/velocity-edits-tutorial/)). Only for the phonk lane.
- **Loop:** last 2 seconds must flow into the first 2; "if your Short ends on a freeze-frame with text, you've killed your loop multiplier" ([Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/gaming-shorts-retention-tips)). YouTube counts every replay as a view since 31 Mar 2025 ([Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/youtube-shorts-looping/)). A tunnel entry→exit that re-enters is the natural Floored loop.
- **Audio:** keep raw engine audio when it carries the moment; layer music only on silent clips ([insights.gg](https://insights.gg/blog/how-to-post-gaming-clips-to-tiktok)). Match the audio peak to a visual cut.
- **Cut the first 1.5s of any underperformer and repost** ([Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/gaming-shorts-retention-tips)).
- Caveat from the 2026 marketing side: creator-led "show, don't tell" clips beat trailer-chops; some argue for **no** text overlay at all ([Cloutboost](https://www.cloutboost.com/blog/tiktoks-changing-landscape-for-game-marketing-in-2026-what-developers-need-to-know)). Test both.

## Bottom line for Floored

1. Lead with the **"is this real"** angle (cockpit, rain, tail lights, engine-only audio) — it's the one hook that works in *both* the car niche and the AI niche, and Bodycam/Shumer/Forza all prove doubt drives the second post.
2. Make "AI wrote it" and "no download" the **slide-2/slide-6 twist**, never the opener.
3. Every set ends on a **one-word question** (cockpit or chase? eurobeat or phonk?) and the answer gets its own post within 24h.
4. Cut every clip so someone else can re-post it: 7–15s, starts moving, loops.
